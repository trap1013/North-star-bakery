The people behind the bread

We follow the
good ingredients.

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: the neighborhood deserves bread made with time, care, and a little bit of wonder.

01 / Our story

A bakery for the neighborhood.

North Star Bakery was created to bring fresh, handmade baked goods to the local community. We make products meant to be shared with family, friends, and the people who happen to be sitting nearby.

02 / Our sourcing philosophy

Simple things, chosen well.

We take care with the ingredients in every bake, supporting quality products whenever possible and letting good flour, butter, fruit, and grain do what they do best.

03 / Our staff

A small team with flour on their aprons.

Our bakery team works side by side to prepare fresh breads, pastries, and cakes while making sure every customer feels welcome at the counter.

04 / Our promise

Leave with something warm.

Whether it is a loaf for dinner or a cake for a milestone, we want each visit to make the day feel a little more generous.
